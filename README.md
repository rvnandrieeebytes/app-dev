# [Me and Thee มีสติหน่อยคุณธีร์](https://www.iq.com/album/me-and-thee-2025-g78hiyaxxh?lang=en_us)
![Me and Thee](https://yt3.ggpht.com/rDuDbRlHo9M-tqvw1HbKfrFoSkhrdym14ulDzmAjjMW8e-t_4sX0db0Q3Kf2-mgznLLSWKA7yQX_=s0)

**Director:**  *X Nuttapong Mongkolsawas*

The story revolves around two very different men:

1.  **Thee** – A wealthy businessman and eldest son of a former mafia family. He's confident, powerful, and used to getting whatever he wants. However, when it comes to romance, he's surprisingly clueless!
2.  **Peach** – A talented freelance photographer who enjoys living a peaceful, quiet life. He's introverted and prefers staying away from unnecessary drama.

Their paths cross when *Thee* develops feelings for *Aran*, a model and *Peach*'s close friend.<br>
Thee then asks *Peach* to teach him how to win *Aran*'s heart.
But here's the twist...<br>
As *Peach* teaches *Thee* about romance, *Thee* gradually realizes that the person he actually wants isn't *Aran* anymore.


![*It's Peach himself!*](assets/peach-text.svg)

And that's when the hilarious romantic chaos begins. 😂❤️
