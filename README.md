# [Me and Thee](https://www.iq.com/album/me-and-thee-2025-g78hiyaxxh?lang=en_us)

The story revolves around two very different men:

1.  **Thee** (*Pond Naravit*) – A wealthy businessman and eldest son of a former mafia family. He's confident, powerful, and used to getting whatever he wants. However, when it comes to romance, he's surprisingly clueless!
2.  **Peach** (*Phuwin Tangsakyuen*) – A talented freelance photographer who enjoys living a peaceful, quiet life. He's introverted and prefers staying away from unnecessary drama.

Their paths cross when *Thee* develops feelings for *Aran*, a model and *Peach*'s close friend.<br>
Thee then asks *Peach* to teach him how to win *Aran*'s heart.
But here's the twist...<br>
As *Peach* teaches *Thee* about romance, *Thee* gradually realizes that the person he actually wants isn't *Aran* anymore.
